PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 12 November 2026

NOT AGAIN: SPIKEY is made by Dacoder637 ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who else handles it, and your choices. Short version: you can play without an account and without telling us anything. If you sign in, we keep your e-mail address, your save and a list of what you bought. Ads are shown by Google, and payments are handled by Flutterwave.

1. Playing as a guest (no account)

Your progress, settings, Bones, death counts, graveyard records, ghost runs and statistics are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. None of it is sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

2. If you sign in (optional)

Signing in lets you back up your progress and use it on another device, and it is required to buy something with real money. It works like this:

  • You give us your e-mail address. We send you a one-time sign-in code. There is no password.
  • We store your e-mail address, your game save (the same progress and settings as in section 1) and a list of your purchases (product, price, date and the payment reference).
  • We use this only to run your account: to sign you in, to back up and restore your save, and to deliver and restore what you bought. We do not send marketing e-mails and we do not sell your data.
  • The account service is Supabase (supabase.com), which stores the data for us on its servers. The sign-in e-mails are sent through Supabase.

3. Payments

Real-money purchases are processed by Flutterwave (flutterwave.com). You pay on Flutterwave's own checkout page, by card, bank transfer, USSD or another method it offers; we never see or store your card or bank details. We pass Flutterwave your e-mail address so it can send your receipt. Flutterwave tells us that a payment succeeded, for which product, the amount and the currency. Flutterwave keeps its own payment records, as the law requires, under its own privacy policy. To show prices in naira or US dollars, the game looks at your device's time zone and language settings; this stays on your device and is not sent to us.

4. Advertising

The game shows optional "watch an ad for a reward" ads and, at most, an occasional ad after you finish a level (never after a death). These ads are provided by Google (AdSense, H5 Games Ads). Google may use cookies or similar technology and information such as your IP address and device type to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure them. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and you can choose non-personalised ads. You can also manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the end-of-level ads.

5. Clips and sharing

When you press SAVE CLIP or SHARE, the game makes a video or picture of the game screen on your device and passes it to your browser's or phone's own save or share feature. The game does not upload it.

6. Rivals codes

A Rivals code contains the name you typed and some game statistics. It is only shared if you copy and send it yourself. Codes you paste in are stored on your device.

7. Hosting

The game's web pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (or the host of the website you play on). Like every web server, the host records technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

8. How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your e-mail address, your online save and your purchase list from our account service straight away. Flutterwave and Google keep their own records under their own policies.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Play as a guest: nothing is sent to us.
  • Delete your account at any time: Settings → Account → "Delete my account". You can also ask us to do it.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it. If you are in the UK or the EEA you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO: ico.org.uk).
  • Deletion and copy requests go to the contact e-mail in section 12 when one is set; when no e-mail is set, use the project page linked there.
  • Delete the progress on one device: Settings → Reset Progress, or clear the site data in your browser.

The legal bases we rely on are: performing our agreement with you (your account, saves and purchases), our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, and your consent where it is needed for ads.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not create an account. If you believe a child has given us their e-mail address, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Important changes will be shown in the game.

12. Contact

Questions, deletion requests or a copy of your data: https://github.com/Dacoder637/not-again-spikey

When a contact e-mail is set it appears here as a mailto link. When it is not set, this section shows the project page instead, so there is always a way to reach us.
